Its sustained repeat reached 0.0929139 pages/s with all 76 source checks passing. A later passive observation found the engine asleep/unloaded 358.184 seconds after the last page under its 300-second idle policy; exact latency and a new Windows Job peak were not measured. The earlier [BF16 C4 active-lease-loss result](../benchmarks/evidence/sycl-normal-service-2026-10-16.json) belongs to the unchanged ownership transport and is not a new C6 test. Dense source failures and `qualified:false` remain explicit in [the engine guide](sycl-engine-trial.md#numerical-and-ocr-scope).
