The CPU recurrence replay then completed in 444.281 seconds with one CPU, 256 MiB and no Torch. Its unchanged one-step screen recorded 72 failed state observations and 11,853 inconclusive output observations, with no output observation classified as failed. Those verdicts remain held: a one-step budget is not an accumulated error bound, and propagated interval uncertainty can become conservative. A stock GPU replay on identical prepared operands and a rigorously bounded reference are being prepared to distinguish arithmetic regression from these limitations. This controlled sequence does not qualify ordinary OCR or throughput.

The throughput objective is correctly completed pages divided by the sustained measured interval at the node’s concurrency plateau. Planned points use two prime, two warmup and sixteen measured pages with one loaded worker. C1 uses the single-row path; C2/C4 supply queued work to FFN batch two. C4 does not mean a four-row GPU batch. Promising points need repeats and adjacent concurrency before establishing the ceiling or a gain. Original pixels, context, output allowance, model revision and BF16 precision remain pinned. Complete OCR quality and numerical error have separate checks; neither kernel probes nor short warm bursts establish node throughput.
